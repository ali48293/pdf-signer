Last updated: June 8, 2026

At SignifyPDF, we believe your privacy is absolute. Our application is built to process PDF documents 100% locally within your web browser. When you use SignifyPDF to view or sign documents, your files are never uploaded, transmitted, or stored on any external servers.

2. Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data, document content, or signatures. All rendering, signing, and saving operations occur directly on your device using JavaScript.
